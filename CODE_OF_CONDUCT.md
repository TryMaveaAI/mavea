# Code of Conduct

This policy governs interaction around Mavéa. It does not invite external code, patches,
documentation changes, or pull requests; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity, and to act in ways that build an
open, friendly, and healthy community.

## Our standards

We expect everyone in this community to:

- Be respectful, kind, and considerate.
- Welcome differing viewpoints, and give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, publishing
others' private information without permission, and any other conduct that would reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Standard adopted

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. Where this summary is silent, that document governs — including its enforcement
guidelines.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within issues, discussions, security-report channels, maintainer pull
requests, and whenever an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at **trymavea@gmail.com**. All
reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainers will respect
the privacy and safety of the reporter.
